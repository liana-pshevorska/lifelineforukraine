This post exists to show the shape of an update and to prove the blog pipeline works.
**Delete it** (or replace its contents) before launch — see `POSTING.md` for how.

An update earns trust when it is specific. The pattern that works:

## What we sent

A plain list of what was purchased, in real quantities.

- 40 CAT tourniquets
- 25 packs of hemostatic gauze
- 10 complete IFAKs

## Where it went

Name the partner or unit if it is safe to do so, and the general area if it is not.
Never publish anything that could identify a position.

## What it cost

Say the number. "100% of donations go to aid" is much more convincing next to an actual
figure than it is on its own.

## What came back

A line from the people who received it, a photo of the delivery, a confirmation. This is
the part donors remember.
