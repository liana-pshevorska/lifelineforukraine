What we send

Not whatever is cheapest, and not whatever was donated to us. We buy the three things that decide whether someone survives the first ten minutes after an injury.

Tourniquets

A limb wound can kill in three minutes. A tourniquet applied in one stops it. These are consumable — once used, they are gone, which is why the requests never stop coming.

Blood-clotting gauze

Hemostatic gauze for the wounds a tourniquet cannot reach: the neck, the shoulder, the groin. It buys the time needed to reach a surgeon.

Individual First Aid Kits

A complete IFAK carried on the body — tourniquet, gauze, chest seal, decompression needle, shears, pressure bandage. One kit, one person, one chance.

How your donation gets there

Four steps, no detours. We are a small organization on purpose — it is why nothing is skimmed off along the way.

  1. 1

    Medics tell us what they need

    We are connected to local doctors and servicemen in Ukraine who communicate their most urgent medical needs directly to us.

  2. 2

    You give

    By PayPal, card, or Zelle. Your gift is tax-deductible and goes into one pool spent only on supplies.

  3. 3

    We buy the exact items requested

    Real medical-grade equipment from suppliers we have vetted — never counterfeit tourniquets, which are common and lethal.

  4. 4

    Trusted partners deliver

    Partners we have worked with since 2022 carry the supplies the last mile, and confirm receipt. Then we publish what happened.
